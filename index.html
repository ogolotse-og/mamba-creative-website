04 / About

Meet Og Ntwaagae.

An all-rounder multimedia designer with a broad production toolkit and international study, work and life experience.

About Og

He comes from Botswana in Southern Africa and is married to a ta'ahine Tonga.

He is an all-rounder Multimedia Designer, with experience in a vast majority of roles including writing, producing, animation, motion graphics, camera work, lighting, editing, graphic design and video editing. He possesses versatile skills and is able to undertake various challenges from a majority of media / communication related matters.

Og has lived, worked and studied in five continents: Malaysia (Asia), Botswana (Africa), Switzerland (Europe), New York (America), Melbourne (Australia) and now resides in Tonga with his wife.
